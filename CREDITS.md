### Changes made for the Dragon Deck

- Each of the 12 court cards has one added layer: a small heraldic dragon perched on the figure (a shoulder, sleeve
  or flower), drawn in the set's own colours (gold `#FC4`, red, blue `#44F`), line weights and engraved line
  shading. Like the rest of each picture, it is drawn once and repeated upside down for the bottom half.
- The back has an added white oval medallion in the centre with two line-work dragons in black ink, one upside
  down, so the back looks the same either way up.
- Only the framed court picture is used; the corner indices are the Dragon Deck's own.
